A new menu and New Year gathering at Central Baku

At the end of 2019 Blogstar held its final event of the year at Central Baku restaurant, together with influencers. The gathering combined the launch of the restaurant's new menu with a New Year celebration.

Presenting the product in a festive setting allowed influencers to experience the new menu in a natural, sincere context, which makes the content shared with audiences more convincing.

Blogstar thanks the influencers and partners who took part.
