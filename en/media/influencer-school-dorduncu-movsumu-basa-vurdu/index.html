Influencer School completes its fourth season

In May 2022 Blogstar Influencer School, the first influencer school in Azerbaijan, completed its fourth season.

The programme aims to turn content creation into a structured profession: participants learn personal branding, content production and working with brands through practical assignments. Each new season is updated on the basis of the agency's real campaign experience.

Blogstar thanks the trainers, participants and partner brands who contributed to the season's success.
