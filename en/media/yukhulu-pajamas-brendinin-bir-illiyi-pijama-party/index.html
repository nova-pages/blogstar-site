Yukhulu Pajamas turns one: a Pyjama Party

On 11 March 2018 Yukhulu Pajamas, the first local pyjama brand, celebrated its first anniversary with a Pyjama Party. Blogstar was the lead organiser.

The concept placed the product at the centre of the event: guests and influencers wore the brand's pyjamas, showcasing the product naturally. This turned the anniversary into both a celebration and an effective product presentation.
